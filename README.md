# Ucolor 

**A little tool for finding the right colors, and a place to make it better together.**

Welcome to Ucolor's public community repository!

Ucolor is a personal project built around a simple idea: finding the right colors for your next design.

Whether you're working on a website, exploring new combinations, or just looking for inspiration, Ucolor aims to make the process a little easier and more enjoyable.


## Your feedback matters

This repository is a place to share your experience with Ucolor. Whether you've found something you love, something that could be improved, or something that simply doesn't work, I'd love to hear about it.

You can contribute by:

- **Reporting bugs** — Found something broken? Let me know!
- **Suggesting features** — Have an idea that could make Ucolor better?
- **Rating Ucolor** — Share your experience and what you think of the tool.
- **Sharing feedback** — Suggestions, criticism, and general thoughts are always welcome.

No feedback is too small. Even the simplest suggestion can help shape what comes next.

## Is Ucolor open source?

Not yet!

Ucolor is currently a closed-source personal project. This repository exists exclusively for community feedback, bug reports, feature suggestions, and discussions.

I'd love to explore making Ucolor open source in the future, but for now, the source code is not publicly available.
